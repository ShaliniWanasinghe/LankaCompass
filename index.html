🚌 Getting Around

Transport Modes

Everything you need to know about getting around Sri Lanka

🚂

Trains

Most scenic and affordable. Book at station counters. Unreserved 3rd class available anytime.

  • ✅ Most scenic experience
  • ✅ Cheapest option
  • ⚠️ Book 1st/2nd class in advance
  • ⚠️ Delays are common
LKR 50 – 1,500
🚌

Buses

Extensive network covering even remote areas. Intercity AC buses are comfortable. Local buses are crowded but cheap.

  • ✅ Widest coverage
  • ✅ Very affordable
  • ⚠️ Can be crowded
  • ⚠️ No fixed schedule on some routes
LKR 30 – 800
🛺

Tuk-tuks

Iconic three-wheelers, perfect for short distances. Negotiate price BEFORE getting in or use the PickMe app.

  • ✅ Available everywhere
  • ✅ Great for short trips
  • ⚠️ Always negotiate fare first
  • ⚠️ Use PickMe app for fair prices
LKR 100 – 2,000
🚕

Taxi / Uber

PickMe and Uber are available in Colombo and major cities. Metered, safe, and transparent pricing.

  • ✅ Air-conditioned
  • ✅ App-based, transparent pricing
  • ✅ Safest option
  • ⚠️ Not available in remote areas
LKR 500 – 10,000
⛵

Ferries & Boats

Available for coastal travel and crossing lagoons. Whale watching boats depart from Mirissa.

  • ✅ Scenic coastal routes
  • ✅ Whale watching tours
  • ⚠️ Season-dependent
  • ⚠️ Book whale watching in advance
LKR 200 – 5,000
🚗

Car / Scooter Rental

Scooters widely available in tourist towns. Cars available with or without a driver (chauffeur recommended).

  • ✅ Maximum flexibility
  • ✅ Reach remote spots
  • ⚠️ Drive on the LEFT side
  • ⚠️ International license required
LKR 2,000 – 12,000/day

⚠️ Stay Safe

Known Scams & Alerts

Real, verified alerts to protect you from common tourist traps in Sri Lanka

🔴 High Risk

Ella Train "Best Seat" Scam

Several websites charge premium prices for "left side" seats on the Colombo–Ella train, claiming they offer the best scenery. This is completely false.

✅ The Truth: The best views are on the RIGHT side of the train when travelling from Colombo to Ella (e.g., Nine Arches Bridge views). No extra charge for any seat selection on government trains.
Report this: Call SLTDA 1912
🔴 High Risk

Fake Train Ticket Websites

Many unofficial websites claim to sell Sri Lanka train tickets online at inflated prices, sometimes with no actual reservation made.

✅ The Truth: The only official train booking sites are railwayticket.expogroup.lk (government), visits.lk (SLTDA approved), and direct station counters. Any other site should be avoided.
Official booking: railwayticket.expogroup.lk
🟡 Medium Risk

Gem Shop Commission Scams

Friendly locals and tuk-tuk drivers may offer to take you to a "family gem shop" or "special government store". They earn commissions and prices are highly inflated.

✅ The Truth: Only buy gems from SLTDA registered shops. Ask for the gem certificate and registration number before purchasing.
🟡 Medium Risk

Tuk-tuk Overcharging

Drivers may quote 3x the normal fare to tourists, especially at tourist spots and airports.

✅ Solution: Use PickMe or Uber apps for transparent, metered pricing. If negotiating, research fair prices for your route beforehand.
🟢 Info

Mobile Data Recharge Trap

Tourists often top up their balance and use data directly — this depletes credit extremely quickly. LKR 1,000 in raw data can be used up in a few hours.

✅ Solution: Always activate a data package after recharging. See our FYK section for recommended packages from Dialog, Mobitel, and Airtel.
🟡 Medium Risk

Fake Accommodation Photos

Some guesthouses use heavily edited or stolen photos online. The actual property may be very different from what's shown.

✅ Solution: Only book through verified platforms (Booking.com, Airbnb) or SLTDA registered guesthouses. Check recent reviews from the past 3 months.

Can I use my foreign credit card at Sri Lanka ATMs?
Finance James K. from UK
✅
Verified Answer: Yes, Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted at ATMs in cities. However, in rural areas and on trains, cash is preferred. Withdraw at ComBank, HNB, or Sampath Bank ATMs which reliably accept international cards. Notify your bank before travel.
Answered by Lanka Compass Team

Which side of the Ella train has better views?
Transport Priya L. from India
✅
Verified Answer: Right side of the train when going from Colombo to Ella. The Nine Arches Bridge, tea plantation drops, and valley views are best seen from the right. Don't pay extra for any "special view seats" — that is a scam.
